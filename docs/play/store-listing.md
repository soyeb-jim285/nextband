**Full description** (4000)
```
Practise all four IELTS tests on your phone and find out what band you would get today, and why.

WRITING
Write Task 1 and Task 2 against the real clock, with a word count and a plan pad. You get a band for each criterion: Task Achievement or Response, Coherence and Cohesion, Lexical Resource, and Grammatical Range and Accuracy. Every grammar and vocabulary mistake is marked in your essay, with the fix and the reason.

SPEAKING
Answer Part 1, the Part 2 cue card and Part 3 out loud, or talk to a live AI examiner who asks follow-up questions like a real one. Your result shows a band per criterion, your transcript, your speaking pace, where you paused, and which words to work on.

LISTENING AND READING
Full 40-question tests in the exam format: every question type, an exam mode that plays the recording once and counts down like the real test, and a practice mode where you can replay and slow down. After each test you see your band, your answers, the transcript or passage with the answer highlighted, and the reason you lost each mark (picked a distractor, right idea but wrong spelling or word limit, and so on).

FULL MOCK TEST
Sit Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking in one go and get an overall band.

DRILLS FROM YOUR OWN MISTAKES
The mistakes you make in your tests become short drills: rewrite the sentence, check yourself, and the ones you still get wrong come back later. A daily streak and gentle reminders (you choose which, and when) help you keep going.

FREE TO START
Try a test as a guest, without an account. A free account keeps your results, your mistakes and your progress across the web and the app.

Academic and General Training are both covered. Scores are an estimate from our AI marking, not an official IELTS result.

NextBand is independent. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the British Council, IDP: IELTS Australia or Cambridge University Press & Assessment. IELTS is a registered trademark of those organisations.
```

## Reviewer account (owner does this once, about 3 minutes)

The agent cannot write to production, so:
1. In the app or on https://nextband.app, sign up with an address whose inbox you can read, e.g. `soyeb.jim+playreview@gmail.com` (Gmail delivers `+` addresses to your inbox), name "Play Review", and a fresh password used nowhere else.
2. Enter the 6-digit code from the email.
3. On https://nextband.app/admin, open that user and turn on **Unlimited** so the reviewer never hits a test limit.
4. Paste the email and password into App access. Keep the account; reviews of later updates use it too.

## Release: closed testing first

A personal developer account created after 13 Nov 2023 must run a **closed test with at least 12 testers opted in for 14 days in a row** before it can apply for production.

1. Testing → Closed testing → Create track (name: `beta`). Countries: Bangladesh (add others later).
2. Testers: an email list (Google accounts). Recruit 15+ so drop-outs don't take you under 12. Share the opt-in link from the track page.
3. Create release → upload `app-release.aab` from the `android-android-v2-latest` GitHub release (signed with the upload key; Play App Signing manages the app key). Release name: `1.0 (<versionCode>)`.
4. Release notes (en-US):
   ```
   First test release of NextBand: Writing, Speaking, Listening and Reading tests with band scores, the live AI examiner, full mock tests, drills from your mistakes, streaks and reminders.
   ```
5. Roll out to closed testing, then wait 14 days with 12+ testers opted in. Ask them to open the app on several days; Play asks about tester engagement when you apply.
6. Dashboard → Apply for production. Answer the questions about the test honestly (what testers did, what you changed).
7. Production: start with a staged rollout (20 %), watch Android vitals and reviews, then 100 %.

Every upload needs a higher versionCode; CI sets it from the run number, so always upload the newest `app-release.aab`.
